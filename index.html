ABOUT ASA

Three friends.
One agricultural vision.

ASA PRODUCTIONS was envisioned by three friends from Kerala who share an interest in agriculture, sustainability, entrepreneurship and community learning.

Our long-term vision is to develop an advanced integrated farm that demonstrates how different agricultural activities can be connected to create a productive and resource-efficient system.

Ashil Economics • Social Work • Research
Srinath Agriculture • Food Technology
Amal English • Social Work
